# Privacy Policy — ZingZing: Astro Jet Arcade

**Last updated: November 13, 2026**

ZingZing: Astro Jet Arcade ("the App") is developed by Hamdi Ulu. This policy explains how the current iOS version of the App handles information.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores your high score, selected language, and sound and music preferences locally on your device. The developer does not receive this locally stored information. Removing the App may remove this local data.

## Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the App can submit your score to its leaderboard. Apple processes Game Center account and leaderboard information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not operate the Game Center service or receive your Apple account credentials.

## Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current iOS version does not include an active advertising or analytics SDK and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. If these features are added in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as needed.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to the developer. Game Center use is subject to the account settings, parental controls, and terms provided by Apple.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the App's features or practices change. For privacy questions, contact **hamdiulu4301@gmail.com**.
